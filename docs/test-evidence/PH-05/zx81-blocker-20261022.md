## Verified in Chromium (still not playable)

1. Core `81_eo` loads; options (`81_joypad_*`) apply.
2. Content `/mit-zx81-dot.p` reaches the core (**2262 bytes**).
3. Log: `eo_loadp injected 2262 bytes; pc=4092 mem[4092]=FD210040` — CRT prologue found and PC set.
4. Framebuffer remains the idle white screen with a single center cell (hash stable across RUN / D-pad).
5. RetroPad B→R / X→NEWLINE / D-pad produce **zero** pixel delta (`menuDelta=0`).
6. `page.keyboard` does not reach the core; keybovl joypad path also does not move Dot.

**Not VERIFIED_PLAYABLE.** Keep EXPERIMENTAL.

## Likely remaining gap

z88dk CRT entry at `$4092` after `eo_loadp` does not yield a stable interactive `getk()` loop under EmulatorJS (display stays K-like; joypad→`PCKeyDown` does not change the frame). Next: native EightyOne harness to compare PC/D_FILE after load, or a pre-rolled `.z81` snap of running Dot.
